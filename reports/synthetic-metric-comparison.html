PsycheTrajectory: measured synthetic results

Saved evaluation · 8 simulated participants · 2 held-out participants · 81 test daily windows · 78 innovations per filter.

Evidence scope

These measurements exercise the research pipeline on synthetic data. They do not measure cognitive-load accuracy, alert precision, meaningful-shift recall, or clinical utility. The app’s 82%, 76%, and 73% values remain illustrative targets.

Observed feature reconstruction error

Lower is better. Values are the report’s mean per-window observed RMSE on scaled features.

95% innovation-region coverage

Reference: 95%. Coverage is descriptive; it does not establish clinical calibration.

Residual lag-1 autocorrelation

Mean absolute within-participant correlation. Lower indicates less remaining serial structure.

One-step prediction error

Same autoencoder coordinates and per-coordinate RMSE. Lower is better. EMA uses α = 0.25.

How to read the comparisons

The saved run favors PCA on reconstruction error and closeness to nominal coverage, and the autoencoder on residual autocorrelation. PCA’s near-zero reconstruction error warrants checking effective rank and dataset simplicity. PCA and autoencoder latent coordinates have different scales: their innovation RMSE values must not be ranked directly. The previous-embedding and exponential-smoothing errors above use the same coordinates and can be compared. No confidence intervals were saved; two held-out simulated participants are insufficient to establish generalization.
